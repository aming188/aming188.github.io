Background

Nadal was born in Mallorca, Spain, on June 3rd 1986. He has a very good relationship with his uncle, Toni Nadal, who introduced him to tennis.

Soon afterwards, Nadal started training seriously in tennis, although he enjoyed soccer as well. He had a successful junior career, and he turned pro in 2001. He soon won his first ATP match while still being a teenager. He progressed very fast in the rankings, and he won his first French Open in 2003.

Nadal has also given back to the community a lot, creating the Rafael Nadal Tennis Academy, which supports aspiring junior players. He has supported many charities throughout the years, most notably during COVID and the 2010 Haiti Earthquake. He has truly been an inspiration to players of all ages, and has been enjoying retirement so far!

Accomplishments

  • Winner of 14 French Open titles
  • Winner of 92 ATP singles titles
  • Holds 2 Olympic Gold medals
  • Over 200 weeks as ATP World No. 1
  • Holds 22 Grand Slam Singles Titles in total
